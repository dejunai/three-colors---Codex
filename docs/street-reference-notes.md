# Geography and business-street reference — 9 September 2026

This is the agreed review stage: a geography proposal and one improved street. The waterfront, extra residents, schedules, and continuous-town conversion remain later work.

## Geography proposal

The wealthy quarter occupies an inland ridge above the town. Pickman Street remains the civic spine. The business district connects ordinary shopping, school pickup, and museum visits; the lower residential quarter has a short working route to the waterfront. A future quay sits below them. The whaling station remains on an offshore island, buried by an unexplained mud flood/slide. Its potential sightline from the quay is background scenery, avoidable and unmarked. The plan does not establish compass bearings or exact distances as canon.

The SVG and PNG are planning artifacts. Dashed roads are proposed future connections; today's hub transitions remain functional. The ridge feeling is recorded for later terrain work, not implemented in the upper district now.

## Implemented visual reference

The business street now uses varied building widths and heights, gabled roofs, chimneys, substantial recessed door surrounds, window frames/sills, timber divisions, awnings, and dark shop-sign fascias. The schoolhouse has a brick frontage and a bench/notice case. Individual paved frontages vary in width along a clear through route with gutters.

Brick, stone, slate, plaster, and wood use small deterministic procedural textures. Textures are shared by surface kind, and materials are cached across visits. There are no external art downloads or copied Gothic assets. This is a first direction toward deliberate low-poly architecture within the existing monochrome presentation, not a finished art pass. The street's underlying alignment remains straight at this stage.

The six businesses, four enterable interiors, two exterior-only doors, interaction destinations, saved world identifiers, and return spawns are retained. Only the business district uses the new geometry module. No new evidence or dialogue was added.
