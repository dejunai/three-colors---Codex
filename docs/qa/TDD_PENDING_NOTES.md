- **BUG (regressed) — camera jumps on random menu closures. RESOLVED, confirmed against source, 2026-09-14.** Reported fixed by the author ("mouse jump id'd and fixed per Codex"); independently confirmed by direct read of `main.gd` (real shell access) rather than taken on report. Root cause, per the fix's own in-source comment, is more specific than either direction speculated in this note's original entry: browsers can report the *entire* pointer movement that happened while the mouse was free — including drift off the game canvas — as one inflated relative-motion delta on the first motion sample after pointer lock re-acquires post-menu-close, and can emit *more than one* such synthetic sample before the next physics frame notices the mouse-mode change. So the prior single-input-swallow guard wasn't reverted — it was never sufficient for a multi-sample burst, confirming the second of the two directions this note originally floated. Fix: `RECAPTURE_MOTION_GUARD_SECONDS = 0.12`, armed via `_arm_mouse_motion_guard()` at the moment `capture_mouse()` re-engages pointer lock (and defensively in `_physics_process()` if `Input.mouse_mode` is ever caught out of sync with the menu state); `_apply_mouse_look()` discards every motion sample for the whole guard window rather than just the first one. New test `tests/camera_recapture_flow.gd`, read directly: confirms a burst of two large relative-motion samples immediately after a menu close is fully suppressed (pitch/yaw unchanged), and that ordinary mouse look resumes once the guard window elapses. **Fold into the next real TDD revision** (this is a single confirmed small fix, not a batch — per the standing instruction at the top of this file, not bumping the version for it alone); note when that happens that TDD v37's Part Six item 19, which logged this as still-open, will need to be marked resolved.
