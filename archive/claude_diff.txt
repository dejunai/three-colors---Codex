+## The corkboard/link mechanic
+
+The single most consequential open item since TDD v1: two coexisting case-tracking systems, a legacy `GameState` autoload implementing a link-based corkboard the active build never used, and `case_state.gd`'s flat evidence-count `perception()`. Resolved by building the real mechanic rather than deleting the legacy one or committing to the flat formula — the short-story drafts (`docs/design/1)` and `4)`) describe Walter's actual method as literally pinning cards and connecting them with twine ("he linked names with twine... discovered... he was good at this"), which is fiction the flat formula was never honoring.
+
+The legacy `GameState.try_link()` was not migrated as code — its `VALID_LINKS` table references an older, smaller evidence catalog (`impossible_wound`, `clean_knife`, `club_token`...) that predates the current build's evidence ids entirely and doesn't correspond to anything in `story.gd`/`town_story.gd`/`tunnel_story.gd`. `GameState` remains legacy, registered only for the retained 2.5D prototype, exactly as before.
+
+The new mechanic instead: `case_state.gd` gained `links: Array[String]`, `record_link(id)`, `has_link(id)`, and CaseState schema bumped to `VERSION = 4` (accepting 1–3 for migration; a save with no `links` key restores with an empty list, no schema-specific migration code needed). `case_state.gd` stays chapter-agnostic on purpose — it only counts confirmed link ids, with no opinion on which pairs are valid.
+
+Which pairs are valid is Chapter One's own content, living in `chapter_one_archive.gd`'s `LINKS` const (six entries, keyed by two evidence ids sorted and joined with `|`) rather than in `case_state.gd` — the same reuse boundary the shared/chapter-specific split already established elsewhere. Five of the six pairs are content that used to be automatic board paragraphs (revealed the instant both evidence ids were present); they're now genuine player-drawn connections, confirmed through `_board()`'s new "Draw a connection between two observations" button into a two-step `_link_picker(g, first_id)` screen. A correct pair calls `state.record_link()` and shows its title/summary permanently under "CONNECTIONS DRAWN" on the board; an incorrect pair records nothing and just says the line doesn't hold — unlike the legacy demo, no instability penalty attaches to a wrong guess, since instability isn't a concept this build's `case_state.gd` has (that stays part of the deferred ontological-break future work). One board paragraph (the gardener/service-door note, keyed to a statement rather than two evidence ids) couldn't convert to this shape and stays automatic.
+
+`perception()` changed shape to make the connection matter, not just decorate it: `2 + min(3, evidence.size()/3) + min(3, links.size())`. A link is worth roughly three observations' worth of raw collection, capped at three links' credit each — full evidence credit alone now needs 9+ observations (up from 8 under the old `2 + min(4, evidence.size()/2)`), while a handful of correct links reaches the same ceiling with far less volume. The service passage's existing `perception() >= 4` gate is untouched and still only reveals descriptive text, never blocks movement or access — confirmed against the existing `tests/interaction_loop.gd` scenario (7 evidence, 0 links → perception 4 under both the old and new formula, coincidentally, so that test needed no changes).
